About

I study when the output of a visual or multimodal model should be trusted, and what to do when it should not. That includes calibrated prediction sets that survive merging and capacity limits, verification procedures for vision-language answers, audits that expose systematic rather than random failure modes, and abstention rules for segmentation models. I care about guarantees that hold under the assumptions a practitioner can actually check.

I am in the final year of the five-year integrated master's (MInf) in Informatics at the University of Edinburgh, after an exchange year at the University of California, Davis. I am applying to PhD programs starting in autumn 2027.
